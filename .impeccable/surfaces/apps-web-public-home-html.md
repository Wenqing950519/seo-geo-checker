## Scope

## Audience and job

Taiwanese small-brand owners and marketers, non-technical, checking whether AI answers mention or cite their site; also LS-Labs readers verifying the research. Action: paste a URL, get a report whose every number opens to evidence.

## Constraints

Keep all JS behaviour, ids and copy that tests lock (custom-query modal, AI 信任值 wording, favicon version). Report copy never names providers. Unknown is not zero. No invented claims.
